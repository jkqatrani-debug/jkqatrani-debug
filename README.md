# Osama S Qatrani

Independent researcher and founder of the **Third Generation Research Initiative (3GIR)**, working across international relations, digital government, artificial intelligence, and algorithmic governance.

## Research interests

- International relations and global power transformations
- Digital government and algorithmic governance
- Artificial intelligence, society, and human identity
- Cybersecurity, digital diplomacy, and geopolitical risk
- Interdisciplinary political and philosophical research

## Academic background

- Master of Arts in International Relations
- Master's degree in Digital Government
- Computing, information technology, and design studies with The Open University
- Doctoral research preparation
